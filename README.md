🌾 MonsterFarm: Harvest, Battle & Thrive!

## MonsterFarm is a thrilling blend of farming simulation and action-adventure. Step into the muddy boots of a brave farmer who must not only grow crops and manage the land—but also defend it from invading monsters. Harvest your way to fortune, fight to survive, and collect coins to upgrade your farm and gear!
🎮 Gameplay Features

    🌽 Harvest Crops
    Plant, water, and harvest a variety of crops. Sell them for coins or use them to trade for special items.

    🧟‍♂️ Fight Monsters
    Defend your farm from wild creatures and boss monsters that appear day and night. Use tools or unlock powerful weapons.

    💰 Collect Coins
    Earn coins from harvesting, defeating enemies, and completing tasks. Use coins to buy seeds, gear, and farm upgrades.

    🛠️ Upgrade & Expand
    Unlock new areas, fortify your farm, and improve your tools and weapons.

## 🕹️ How to Play

    Plant Crops: Select seeds from your inventory and plant them in your fields.

    Harvest: Wait for the crops to grow and harvest them to earn coins.

    Combat: When monsters appear, use your weapon or farming tools to fight them off.

    Collect Coins: Gather coins from selling crops or defeating monsters.

    Upgrade: Use your earnings to expand your farm or improve your combat gear.
